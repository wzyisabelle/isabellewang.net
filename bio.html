iSABELLE WANG
Index Bio

Isabelle Wang is a creative technologist and computational artist based in London and Shanghai. She recently graduated with an MFA in Computational Arts from Goldsmiths, University of London.

Working across interactive installations, machine vision, creative coding, and immersive environments, she explores how technology can be used creatively to design engaging and meaningful experiences for audiences.

With a background in visual communication and experience in the creative industry, she brings a strong visual sensibility and an understanding of brand storytelling and commercial creative strategies.

She remains curious about how emerging technologies can reshape the way people perceive the physical world. She believes technology is most meaningful when it creates something beyond itself: a moment of genuine human experience and connection.
